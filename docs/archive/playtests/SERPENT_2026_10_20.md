# Serpent evasion verification — 20 October 2026

Historical verification receipt for PT2-12. Current rules belong in `docs/CURRENT_GAME.md`; this is not a separate design brief.

## Decision and implementation

The player accepted retaining 20% ordinary / 30% Apex and World Serpent innate evasion, with little or no recoil bonus. The implemented choice removes the recoil bonus entirely. Intent recoil still retreats one range band; Pin still prevents movement and halves dodge; Spotter Flare suppresses dodge. The legacy AI no longer chooses the former evasion-building special action and falls through to an attack instead. The HUD reads the same effective dodge value as combat, including 0% under Flare.

Before this fix, repeated forced Apex venom recoils produced 30 → 50 → 70 → 80 → 80% dodge, which remained at 80% after an ordinary action. An 85%-accuracy shot then connected only 17% of the time. No duplicate ship-dodge roll was found against monsters.

## Deterministic coverage

New core tests exercise 12 consecutive alternating venom/fanged recoils for ordinary, Apex and World Serpents, preserving innate dodge and retreat. Pin blocks retreat, expires after the monster turn and restores innate dodge; Flare expiration restores the appropriate pinned/unpinned chance. The old trait special entry point cannot accumulate dodge. The legacy AI attack regression verifies it does not waste the turn on that removed bonus.

Actual attack resolution enumerates all 10,000 accuracy/dodge roll pairs per case for a fresh, unblessed Standard Broadside at Medium:

| Target | Normal connection | Pinned connection | Spotter Flare connection |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary Serpent | 68% | 76.5% | 95% |
| Apex / World Serpent | 59.5% | 72.25% | 95% |

These combine independent accuracy and dodge rolls. They are not weapon accuracy labels; Long cannon penalties, obscurity and other modifiers still affect the final connection rate.

The Flutter test at 115% text verifies the Apex HUD transitions through 30%, 15%, 0% and back to 30% as Pin/Flare change, using controller-driven rebuilds and disabled autosave.

## Later correction — inherited UI fixtures were not fully isolated

An audit on 20 October 2026 found older UI/art fixtures with default autosave and no injected storage. Full app test runs that included those fixtures could overwrite production profiles with fixture data, including unpaid blueprint unlocks. Earlier blanket statements that verification left production saves untouched were not established for those runs; the passing test counts above do not prove isolation. The specialized tests that explicitly disabled autosave/injected storage remain distinct from that inherited full-suite risk. The fixtures are now corrected and a tested per-suite disposable storage fallback protects future default controllers. See the test-save isolation and battle-art receipt in the archive index and PT2-27 in the live backlog. No historical test result is being relabeled as a storage-isolation check.
