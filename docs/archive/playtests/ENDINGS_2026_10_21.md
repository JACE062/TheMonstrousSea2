## State and checkpoint behavior

Core terminal phases remain `ended` and `death`, preserving headless consumers. `AcknowledgeCalderaOutcome` changes only the read flag; the app gates those phases on the unread outcome. No additional ending rewards or economic changes were introduced.

The controller now checkpoints the pre-choice `expeditionVictory` phase and retains a resolved-outcome checkpoint. Optional choice/read fields and the exact offered heirs are serialized by `VoyageSaveData`, with absent fields retaining old-save defaults. Completed writes restore the current ending without invoking resolution again. Claim's checkpoint persists through acknowledgement and clears when an heir is chosen; Shatter stays complete across restart. Older shattered-Font profiles without a checkpoint open a completed hunt rather than a dead opening menu. Ordinary deaths and home preparation keep their existing checkpoint-deletion rules.

These regressions verify completed saves/reloads and duplicate command rejection; they do not establish atomicity of the existing separate profile/checkpoint writes under process termination midway through I/O.
