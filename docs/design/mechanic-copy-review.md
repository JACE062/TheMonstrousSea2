**Status: D01–D44 selected and implemented.** D01–D02 shipped first; the subsequent approvals and five clarification answers complete D03–D44. The original review tables below retain the before/proposal comparison; the selection notes record amendments to those proposals. These IDs are separate from the completed C01–C36 [earlier copy review](player-copy-review.md). Within that D review, the sole approved combat change is D33: Tidal Shockwave now resolves as Blast, including its Illuminated interaction. Other D changes are copy, help or corrections to displayed values. The later E review below includes the user's all-movement Grappled clarification and its missing Ram guard.

The [player report](../playtests/2026-11-08/mechanic-copy/01-player.png) asks descriptions to explain what their own action does, with condition definitions on the condition's tooltip. Keep actual bonuses, penalties, duration, cooldown, targeting, movement and prerequisites. Mentioning an applied condition such as Burning is appropriate; explaining that condition or listing other things that interact with it is a separate question. The review includes borderline **keep** recommendations so the user can decide rather than treating every mention of another mechanic as clutter.

`N`, `A`, `B` and similar placeholders below mean the current displayed, fitting-derived value. They are not proposed tuning. Tier-dependent effects must remain tier-dependent. The tables preserve the review draft; selected changes and the current rules take precedence over its original alternatives.

## Reviewed and recommended to retain

- Alchemical Pitch's current belt effect **applies Burn**; Unstable Tincture's three possible conditions; Chain-Shot's **bonus against Pinned**; Iron Constitution's **Burn/Poison mitigation**; and Daredevil's **hull threshold** are effects or prerequisites of those choices. They are not automatically clutter just because another mechanic is named.
- Relics' distinct penetration, healing from flesh damage, obscurity bypass, Shock riders, Frenzy disruption, Burn and Poison applications are their own effects. Keep those summaries and their conditions; none currently defines an entire unrelated status rule.
- Covenant modifiers and monuments principally state their own penalties/bonuses. Keep the actual condition being modified. Keep prices, permanence, one-use limits, irreversible-choice/save safeguards and immediate availability reasons.
- Current Dodge/Evade help is the appropriate place for avoidance rules and the 95% ship cap, as explicitly requested. Existing Burn/Poison/Stun, Anchored and Frenzy tooltips principally explain their own effects. Corroded's missing amplification amount is called out under D19.
- Descriptive item flavor, the authored prologue/endings and blueprint identity prose remain outside this mechanics-copy sweep. Retired weapons and unreachable lore-table tactical tips are not promoted into live requirements.

## Linked mechanic words — implemented interface

Gold mechanic words open parchment explanations on hover, activation or long press. Nested terms open their own card and keep the preceding card available; cards use available space and remain within the viewport at compact widths/large text. Keyboard focus, F1 for action details, Escape/outside/assistive dismissal and screen-reader activation are supported. Help on an action does not dispatch it. Combat help reads detached displayed actors; shrine comparison help reads the prospective dedication without changing the build.

This is linked contextual help, not a separate encyclopedia screen. Existing item flavor and story prose remain outside the sweep. No artwork or dependency was added.
