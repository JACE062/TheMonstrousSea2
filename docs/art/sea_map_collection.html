The Monstrous Sea

Simple ink-and-parchment collection: 5 vessels, 6 creatures, and 12 equipment and utility icons, and 3 chart markers. The previous freighter is restored. The rounded squid has underside suckers, and the tincture contains an abstract roiling mixture. Ships and weapons face right; sea enemies approach from the right and face left.
